# Profile: music-montage

Events, aftermovies, real estate, places and products with little or no speech.

- **How**: `montage.py` — moments from the picture, chosen by looking at their sheets, cut on
  the beat of the track: **`references/montage.md`**.
- **Structure**: follows the music — intro, build-up, peak, close.
- **Pacing**: cuts on the beats; shot length follows the track's energy (every 2 beats when
  it's loud, a bar or two when it's quiet).
- **Cleanup**: shot selection instead — drop blown, shaky or repeated takes.
- **Music**: a real track — it's the backbone; choose it first. Ambient sound only as accents.
- **Subtitles**: usually none; text only as titles or info cards.
- **Graphics**: usually minimal — the music is the sound design, no effects over it; the facts the viewer needs (event name/date,
  location, prices or features in real estate), in the video's style.
- **Picture**: a shaky take worth keeping gets `stabilize`; match color per place.
- **Traps**: a cut that misses the beat is very noticeable; check sync after any
  re-encode.
- **Color**: Basic plus a look is expected (this type is judged on looks): suggest `cinematic` as Custom unless the brief says otherwise.
