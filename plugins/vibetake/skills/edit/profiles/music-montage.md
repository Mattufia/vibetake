# Profile: music-montage

Events, aftermovies, real estate, places and products with little or no speech.

- **Structure**: follows the music — intro, build-up, peak, close.
- **Pacing**: **cuts on the beats** (detect beats; vary between every beat and
  every 2–4 beats as the energy changes).
- **Cleanup**: shot selection instead — drop blown, shaky or repeated takes.
- **Music**: it's the backbone; choose it first. Ambient sound only as accents.
- **Subtitles**: usually none; text only as titles or info cards.
- **Graphics**: event name/date, location, prices or features (real estate) as
  clean cards.
- **Picture**: stabilize, match color between clips; this type is judged on looks.
- **Traps**: a cut that misses the beat is very noticeable; check sync after any
  re-encode.
- **Color**: Basic plus a look is expected (this type is judged on looks): suggest `cinematic` as Custom unless the brief says otherwise.
- **Music**: a real track (it is the backbone); cut on its beats (`musicgen.py` writes `.beats.json` for generated beds).
