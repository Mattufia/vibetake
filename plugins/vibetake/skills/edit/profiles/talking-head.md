# Profile: talking-head

Opinion, educational or explainer content spoken to camera by one person.

- **Structure**: strongest idea first (consider a cold open in a feed), then
  development, then a closing line or CTA.
- **Pacing**: tight; the speech carries the video.
- **Cleanup**: full — fillers, retakes (keep the last good take), false starts.
- **Music**: optional, low bed; never competing with the voice.
- **Subtitles**: burned-in for feed; `.srt` only for chosen viewing with clean audio.
- **Graphics**: keywords on screen as punchlines; lower third if the person needs
  an introduction.
- **Jump cuts**: always covered with a punch-in (alternate framings) or b-roll.
- **Traps**: over-cleaning removes the person's natural rhythm; keep pauses that
  carry meaning.
- **Color**: Full is usually the recommendation: skin is what's judged. `natural` look at most.
