# Profile: vlog

Day in the life, travel, behind the scenes.

- **Structure**: follows the day or the trip; a hook up front (best moment
  teaser) if it's going to a feed.
- **Pacing**: varied — quick montages between locations, room to breathe when
  something happens.
- **Cleanup**: light; spontaneity is the point. Remove dead time, not
  personality.
- **Music**: changes by scene or location; ambient sound kept under it.
- **Subtitles**: depends on destination; ambient-heavy audio often needs them.
- **Graphics**: dynamic but light, in the creator's style: location titles, a few keywords, whooshes on location changes.
- **Stabilize** handheld shots that need it.
- **Traps**: sponsor segments get the `ad` treatment (see mixed material).
- **Color**: Basic, one match group per place (each place keeps its own light — the beach and the kitchen
  shouldn't look alike); a look only if the creator has a style, and then it's what ties the day together.
