# Profile: tutorial

Screencasts and step-by-step instructional videos.

- **Structure**: what you'll achieve → steps in order → result. Chapters per step
  if it runs past ~5 minutes.
- **Pacing**: calmer than a feed default; cut waiting (loading, typing, renders)
  but **never cut a step** the viewer needs to reproduce.
- **Cleanup**: medium — fillers and dead time, not explanations.
- **Music**: none or a very low bed.
- **Subtitles**: `.srt` by default; burned-in only for feed.
- **Graphics**: zoom into the area where the action happens, highlight the
  cursor/click, step number on screen, callouts for shortcuts and values.
- **Traps**: on-screen text (UI, code, values) must stay legible after any
  reframe or zoom; a vertical crop of a screen recording is usually unreadable —
  say so and propose zoom-ins instead.
- **Color**: level 1 on the camera shots only; never grade screen recordings (UI colors must stay true).
