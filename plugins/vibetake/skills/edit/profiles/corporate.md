# Profile: corporate

Company presentations, institutional videos, internal communication.

- **Structure**: who we are → what we do → proof (clients, numbers, team) → close
  with brand and contact.
- **Pacing**: measured, professional; no aggressive jump cuts.
- **Cleanup**: full but discreet — no visible cuts in shots of executives.
- **Music**: neutral corporate bed, low.
- **Subtitles**: `.srt` always; burned-in if it goes to social media.
- **Graphics**: strict brand compliance (logo, colors, typefaces); lower thirds
  with name and role.
- **Traps**: names, roles and figures are checked letter by letter; nothing
  off-brand on screen.
